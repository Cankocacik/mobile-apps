A little order, one color at a time.

Sort Pop is a ball sort puzzle for short, unhurried breaks. Move colorful balls between tubes until each tube holds one color. Plan a few moves ahead, try a new route and enjoy putting the board in order.

Status

Sort Pop is still in development and is not in the stores yet. Android is first; an iPhone version is planned. The game plays in English and Turkish. The screenshots on this English page show the English UI.

Made to feel calm, not crowded

Every level has a solution verified by a solver. Here is what you get.

  • 50 verified levels

    Each of the 50 starting boards has a solution verified by a solver. If your moves lead to a dead end, undo or restart and try another route.

  • Two taps to move

    Tap a tube, then tap where it should go. The top group of matching balls moves together. A tube is done when it holds four balls of one colour.

  • Free undo and restart

    Undo as often as you like and restart any level whenever you want. There is no timer, no lives, no energy and no streak to protect.

  • Hints that really help

    Every attempt includes one free hint. Hints point at a real next move that can still be solved, never a random guess.

  • Symbols alongside color

    Colour symbols on the balls help colour-blind players. Sound, haptics and motion each have their own switch.

  • Offline, saved on your phone

    Play anywhere without a connection. Your progress is saved on the device, so you resume right where you left off.

How it plays

You can learn it in one level.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Tap a tube

    Pick the tube whose top balls you want to move.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Tap a destination

    Drop them on an empty tube or on a matching colour. The whole matching group moves together.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Fill every tube with one colour

    Four balls of the same colour per tube finishes the level. Stuck? Undo, restart or use a hint.

Ads and optional Premium, plainly

The game is free to play and supported by ads. Here is exactly how that works.

  • Your first five level completions are completely ad-free.
  • After that, an ad may appear when you move on to the next level. Never in the middle of a puzzle.
  • A rewarded video is only ever your choice, for an extra hint.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you see a consent form first.
  • An optional one-time Premium purchase removes all ads, including rewarded videos, and gives you three ad-free hints per level attempt. Undo stays free for everyone.

Your progress stays on your phone

There is no account, no sign-up and no server for your game. The game saves your progress on your device. The only outside services are the ads described above and, if you choose it, the store purchase.

  • No account or sign-up
  • Progress saved on the device
  • Ad consent shown where required

Questions

Is Sort Pop out yet?

Not yet. It is still in development. This page will link to the store listing once it exists.

Is it free?

Yes, the game is free to play and supported by ads (see “Ads and optional Premium” above). An optional one-time Premium purchase removes all ads and gives you three ad-free hints per level attempt. There is no subscription.

How many levels are there?

Fifty, each one checked by a solver to have a solution. There are no daily challenges or endless modes.

Do I need an internet connection?

The puzzles work offline. Ads and optional store purchases need a connection.

Can I play in another language?

The game plays in English and Turkish.

Will I lose progress if I close the app?

No. Progress is saved on your device and the game resumes where you left off. There is no cloud save, so progress does not move between phones.
