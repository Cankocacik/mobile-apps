DueNook — Privacy Policy

DueNook Privacy Policy — Effective: 12 November 2026 — Developer: Muhammed Can Kocacık — Contact: kcck517@gmail.com

In short. DueNook (Turkish name: Ödeme Defteri) is a personal payment notebook that works offline, with no account. Nothing you write is sent to the developer or to any third party. The app does not even request the internet permission.

1. What data is processed? Only what you enter: record and plan names, amounts, currency, dates, notes, your chosen country/language/time zone, your reminder settings, and the receipt/contract files (photos or PDFs) you attach. These files may contain other people's information (for example a landlord's name or IBAN); you add them and they stay on your phone.

2. Where is it stored? Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We run no server; there is no account, sign-in or cloud sync. Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app, so your data leaves the phone only when you choose.

3. Third parties. The app contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting, tracking or any other third-party software development kit (SDK) that collects data. It does not use the advertising ID. No data is sold, rented or shared.

4. Permissions. Notifications: requested when you turn reminders on; reminders are created on your phone and sent nowhere. Camera and photos: the app requests no camera or storage permission; taking a photo uses the system camera app and choosing a file uses the system picker, and only the file you pick is copied into the app. It uses the "run at startup" permission to set reminders up again after the phone restarts.

5. Sharing and backup. A receipt, summary PDF or backup file leaves your phone only when you tap share/save, and goes to the app you choose; what happens next is up to that app and you. The encrypted backup file (.odyedek) is created with your passphrase; the passphrase is stored nowhere and cannot be recovered if you forget it. Temporary copies made for sharing are deleted at the next app start once they are more than an hour old.

6. Deletion and retention. There is no account, so there is no account deletion. You can permanently delete records inside the app; uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the phone. The developer has nothing to delete because no data ever reaches the developer. Files you shared earlier (for example a PDF you sent to another app) are outside this app.

7. Children. The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children (it collects data from no one).

8. Security. Data is protected by the operating system's app isolation and your device lock; the database and receipt files are not additionally encrypted on disk. Backup files are encrypted with Argon2id + ChaCha20-Poly1305. Protect your phone with a screen lock.

9. Not an official document. The records and the summary PDF are your own notes; they are not bank verification, a receipt, or confirmation from the other party.

10. Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page; if data practices change, the app update will say so.
