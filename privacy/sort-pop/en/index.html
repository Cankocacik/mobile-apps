Sort Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026
Publisher / data controller: Muhammed Can Kocacık (individual developer), Türkiye

The game and local data

Sort Pop is a ball-sort puzzle for a mixed audience, including children and older players. No game account is required. Game progress, the current puzzle, undo history, settings and language preferences are stored on the device. Progress does not sync through a developer-operated server.

The app does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location. The developer does not operate an analytics backend. Local diagnostics and random installation/session identifiers used by the app's local telemetry are not uploaded. Third-party advertising and platform purchases involve separate processing described below; this policy does not claim that the entire app collects no data.

Children and advertising

The app uses Google AdMob. The app's audience is mixed. There is no age screen, so the same restrictive advertising settings apply to everyone: child-directed and under-age-of-consent request tags, a G content-rating request cap and non-personalized requests. The Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions are removed from the app. No third-party mediation networks are configured. These controls limit what is requested; they are not a guarantee about every ad delivered by a network.

Google's advertising SDK can process technical data such as IP address, information derived from IP, app and ad interactions, device and app information and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is processed. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Interstitial ads are limited to transitions between completed levels. The first five unique level completions are ad-free. Optional rewarded ads are offered only when a player asks for an additional hint. Network or ad failures do not prevent offline puzzle play. Premium users are not shown interstitial or rewarded ads.

Where applicable, the Google User Messaging Platform handles privacy notices and choices. The messages and choices available depend on the audience configuration, region and SDK state. The game remains playable when ads cannot be requested. Privacy options can be reviewed in Settings when the SDK makes them available.

Optional Premium purchase

Premium is an optional, one-time, non-consumable purchase that removes ads and provides three hints per level attempt. It is not a subscription. Google Play processes payment; the developer does not receive payment-card details. The app queries purchase ownership and stores a purchase-status record (product, platform, transaction information and verification time) locally in secure storage so an existing purchase works offline. Restore purchases queries the same Google Play account.

Support and ad reports

If you choose to send a support email or report an ad, we receive the email address, message and information you send. The ad-report action prepares information such as ad format, the SDK response identifier, app version and platform for your review in your email app; it never sends an email silently. Support correspondence is used only to answer the request and to meet related obligations. Please do not send sensitive information, or information about a child that is not needed to resolve the issue. Parents or guardians can use the contact above for privacy questions.

Choices, removal and contact

You can change sound, haptics, motion, color-symbol and language preferences in Settings. Local progress is removed with the device's app-data controls or by uninstalling; uninstalling does not cancel a permanent store purchase, which you can restore from Google Play. For data processed by Google, see Google's privacy controls.

For questions or privacy requests, contact kcck517@gmail.com. Changes to this policy are published on this page with an updated effective date. Any future analytics, SDK or audience change is reviewed together with this policy and the store disclosures before release.
