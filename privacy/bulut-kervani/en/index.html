Bulut Kervanı — Privacy Policy

Bulut Kervanı Privacy Policy — Effective: 15 November 2026 — Developer: Muhammed Can Kocacık (individual developer), Türkiye — Contact: kcck517@gmail.com

In short. Bulut Kervanı (Turkish name: Bulut Kervanı) is a single-player game that works offline, with no account. In this version no data is sent to the developer or to any third party. Your game progress stays on your phone. The app asks for no permissions.

1. What data is processed? Only what is created while you play: your game progress (your harbor, goods, quests, settings), your language and sound preferences, and any nickname-style information you choose to enter in the game, such as a captain name. We do not ask for or read your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

2. Where is it stored? Only on your phone, in the app's private storage, in a local save file. We run no server; there is no account, sign-in or cloud sync. The developer cannot access this save file. If your phone's own backup settings (for example Android's automatic backup) are turned on, the operating system may back up the app's data to your account; that depends on your device settings and is independent of the developer.

3. Third parties. In this version the app contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting, tracking or any other third-party software development kit (SDK) that collects data. There are no in-app purchases. The advertising ID is not used. The game is built with the Unity game engine; in this version Unity's advertising, analytics and cloud services are not used. No data is sold, rented or shared. If you install the app from Google Play, store-level data that Google collects (for example install counts and crash reports from users who choose to share them) is governed by Google's policies; the developer can see it only as aggregate statistics.

4. Permissions. In this version the app requests no device permissions (location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, notifications and so on). This version does not send or receive data over the internet.

5. Deletion and retention. There is no account, so there is no account deletion. To delete your game data, clear the app's data in your phone's app settings or uninstall the app; the save file is then removed from the phone. The developer has nothing to delete because no data ever reaches the developer.

6. Age and children. The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children (it collects data from no one).

7. Security. Data is protected by the operating system's app isolation and your device lock; the save file is not additionally encrypted on disk. Protect your phone with a screen lock.

8. Test version. This version is an early preview distributed in a Google Play closed test. The game's content and save format may change in later versions; that does not by itself change the data practices described on this page.

9. Changes. If ads or in-app purchases are added to the game in the future (for example optional rewarded ads or cosmetic items), this policy will be updated and the related choices (for example ad consent) will be presented to you in the app. The new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date; if data practices change, the app update will say so.
