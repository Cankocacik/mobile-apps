Skypost Caravan — Privacy Policy

Skypost Caravan Privacy Policy — Effective: 18 November 2026 — Developer: Muhammed Can Kocacık (individual developer), Türkiye — Contact: kcck517@gmail.com

In short. Skypost Caravan (Turkish title: Bulut Kervanı) is a single-player game with no account, and it can be played offline. Your game progress stays on your phone; the developer runs no server and receives no data from you. The game has optional rewarded ads; they are served by Google AdMob, and for that some data is sent from your phone to Google (section 2 below). The game has one-time purchases; Google Play takes the payment and your payment details stay with Google (section 5). You can change your ad choices at any time inside the game, under Settings › Privacy options.

1. What data does the game itself process? Only what is created while you play: your game progress (your harbor, goods, quests, settings), your language and sound preferences, any nickname-style information you choose to enter in the game such as a captain name, which items you own, and how many rewarded ads you watched that day. All of this stays on your phone. We do not ask for or read your real name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos.

2. Ads and our advertising partner. The only ads in the game are rewarded ads: no ad opens unless you tap an ad button. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play on their own. Ads are served by Google AdMob, Google's advertising service. For this, Google's advertising software runs inside the game and sends the following data from your phone to Google:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID (section 4).
  • IP address: it may be used to estimate your general location (country or city level). The game does not get your precise location and does not ask for the location permission.
  • App interactions: information such as the game being opened, an ad being shown, a tap on an ad and an ad video being watched.
  • Diagnostic information: performance information about the app and the advertising software, such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage.

Google uses this data to show ads, to measure and count ads, to prevent fraud and to improve its service. If you give consent, Google also uses it to show you ads personalized to your interests. This data does not reach the developer; in the AdMob dashboard the developer sees only aggregate figures (how many ads were shown, from which countries, how much revenue was earned) and cannot see individual players. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Consent and withdrawing consent. In regions where the law requires consent (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you see a consent form before any ad is shown. The form is provided by Google's consent tool. Outside these regions no form is shown and you are only shown non-personalized ads. In it you can give consent to personalized ads, decline, or make detailed choices; the form also lists the advertising technology providers Google works with.

  • If you do not consent, the game works in full. When you choose to watch an ad, you are shown a non-personalized ad. In that case limited data such as your IP address is still sent to Google so that the ad can be delivered and counted and fraud can be prevented. In some countries no ad may be shown at all without consent; the ad button then says that no ad is available right now, and you lose nothing.
  • To withdraw or change your consent: open Settings › Privacy options in the game. The same form opens again and you can change your choice. Whenever this option is visible to you, you can use it at any time; withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.
  • If you never want to watch ads, you do not need to do anything: no ad opens unless you tap an ad button. The main story can be played without watching ads.

Your consent choice is stored on your phone and is passed to Google together with ad requests.

4. Advertising ID. The Android advertising ID is a number on your phone that is used for advertising and that you can reset. The game itself does not read or store this number; Google's advertising software reads it. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings: on Android, Settings › Privacy › Ads (on some phones Settings › Google › Ads) › "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID". If you delete the ID, the advertising software sees a string of zeros in its place and you are not shown personalized ads based on the ID.

5. Purchases. The game offers one-time purchases (currently only "Ad-Free Rewards"; cosmetic items may be added later). Payment is made through Google Play. Your card number, payment method, billing address and purchase history stay with Google; the game and the developer do not see them. Google Play gives the game only the fact that this Google account owns a given item, together with the verification record of the purchase. The game checks this on your phone, stores your entitlement on your phone and sends it nowhere else; the developer runs no server. If you change phones or reinstall the game, the "Restore purchases" option in the game reads your entitlements from Google Play again on a phone signed in to the same Google account. Refunds and payment problems are handled under Google Play's rules: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637 — How Google Play processes payment data is described in Google's privacy policy.

6. Where is data stored? Your game progress and your entitlements are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, in a local save file. There is no account, sign-in or cloud sync; the developer cannot access this file. If your phone's own backup settings (for example Android's automatic backup) are turned on, the operating system may back up the app's data to your Google account; that depends on your device settings and is independent of the developer. Ad-related data (section 2) is processed not on your phone but on Google's servers; these servers may be outside Türkiye and outside the country where you live, for example in the United States.

7. Third parties. Two third-party services in the game receive data, and both are Google's: Google AdMob for ads and the consent form, and Google Play Billing for purchases. Apart from these there is no analytics, crash reporting, tracking or other advertising network software. The game is built with the Unity game engine; Unity's advertising, analytics and cloud services are not used. The developer does not sell or rent any data. When you install the app from Google Play, store-level data that Google collects (for example install counts and crash reports from users who choose to share them) is governed by Google's policies; the developer can see it only as aggregate statistics.

8. Permissions. The app requests no device permission that needs your approval (location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, notifications). It uses the following permissions, which are granted automatically at install: internet access and viewing network state (for ads and purchases), access to the advertising ID (for the advertising software), and Google Play billing (for purchases). The game can be played without internet; the internet is used only while watching an ad, in the consent form and during a purchase.

9. Retention and deletion.

  • Data on your phone: There is no account, so there is no account deletion. To delete your game data, clear the app's data in your phone's app settings or uninstall the app; the save file and your consent choice are removed from the phone. Items you have bought remain recorded in your Google account and can be restored.
  • Data held by the developer: None. Because no personal data of yours reaches the developer, the developer has nothing to delete.
  • Ad data held by Google: Google holds this data and decides how long it is kept. You can limit its use by deleting your advertising ID (section 4), by withdrawing your consent in the game (section 3) and by using Google's ad settings page (https://myadcenter.google.com). To ask Google for deletion of or access to your data, use the routes described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Purchase records: They are kept in your Google Play account; Google's rules apply.

10. Age and children. The app is intended for players aged 13 and over; it is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect data from children; the game does not ask for age, name or contact details. If the age at which you can give consent on your own is higher than 13 in the country where you live (in some countries it is as high as 16), consent to ads must be given by your parent or guardian. A parent or guardian who believes that a child under 13 is using the game can write to the developer at the address below; the developer holds no data about the child, but we will explain how to delete the data on the phone and the advertising ID. For purchases, Google Play's parental approval and purchase authentication settings can be used.

11. Your rights. Under the laws of the country where you live (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698; in Europe, the General Data Protection Regulation), you have rights to request information about your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its processing and to withdraw consent you have given. You can withdraw your consent inside the game (section 3). Because the developer holds no data that identifies you, most of these rights are exercised against Google, which processes the ad data; Google's privacy policy explains how to contact Google. You can reach the developer with any question at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.

12. Security. Data on your phone is protected by the operating system's app isolation and your device lock; the save file is not additionally encrypted on disk. Protect your phone with a screen lock. Data that the advertising software sends to Google is encrypted in transit.

13. Changes. If a new advertising partner or a new feature that uses data (for example an online account or cloud backup) is added to the game, this policy will be updated beforehand and the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. If data practices change, the app update notes will say so as well.
