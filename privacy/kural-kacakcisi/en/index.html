Rule Smuggler Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026
Publisher / data controller: Muhammed Can Kocacık (individual developer), Türkiye
Contact: kcck517@gmail.com
App: Rule Smuggler (Turkish name: Kural Kaçakçısı), Android package com.rulesmuggler.game

Short version

  • The game has no account, no sign-in, no server of ours and no analytics of our own. We do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • The first hint of every mission is free. A second hint can be unlocked by choosing to watch an optional rewarded video. These ads are provided by Google (AdMob, Google Mobile Ads SDK), which processes some device data to serve and measure ads. There are no banner, interstitial or app-open ads, and the rules and help stay free.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • Where the law requires it, the app asks for your ad-consent choice through Google's User Messaging Platform and lets you change it later.

Data stored on your device

The app stores game progress and your selected language in its local storage on your device: completed missions, best results, experiment counts, whether help was used, unfinished attempts with their chosen shapes and experiment history, which missions have the second hint unlocked, and local completion records (mission and content versions, start and completion times, replay status, whether an attempt began from an invite; at most the latest 500). Your device language chooses the first interface language if you have not saved one. These records are not sent to us.

Automatic Android backup is turned off for this app and its data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so we cannot restore it for you. "Reset progress" removes saved progress and its local history but keeps the language preference. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Advertising: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and consent

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK only to show rewarded videos that you choose to watch for the second hint. No other ad network or mediation partner is used. Each time the app starts it asks Google's User Messaging Platform whether a consent message is needed; an ad is requested only when you open a mission's first hint (so a video can be ready) or tap the button to watch one. According to Google's disclosure for the Android SDK, the SDK collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), device and app identifiers (including the Android advertising ID where available), product interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, and diagnostic information. This data is encrypted in transit. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not give the SDK your name, email address or other identifying details. Ad content is limited to Google's "Teen" maximum content rating.

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choices and your device settings. You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID in Android settings. Where a consent message is required in your region you see it on first launch; where a privacy-options entry is required, an "Ad privacy choices" button in the help window lets you review or withdraw your choice. If you do not consent where consent is required, no ad is requested and the second hint stays locked; the game remains fully playable with the free first hint and the free rules.

Invites and sharing you choose

When you share a mission, the app opens the system share sheet or, if sharing is unavailable, copies the invite link. The invite contains the mission identifier and version only, not your solution, history, score or any identifier of you. The app or person you choose handles what you send under their own policies; the game never sends invites by itself.

Permissions the Android app requests

Internet and network state (to load the optional rewarded videos and the consent message), and the advertising-ID, ad-services, wake-lock and foreground-service permissions declared by Google's ads libraries. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Sharing, retention and your choices

We do not sell your data and do not run our own servers. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is shared with Google, which may process it in other countries under its own safeguards and retains it under its own policies. Data on your device stays until you reset progress, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the Turkish KVKK) you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, the practical controls are the in-app Ad privacy choices (where shown), your device's advertising-ID controls, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, Google's own privacy tools and writing to kcck517@gmail.com. If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer and keep them only as long as needed. You may also complain to your data-protection authority.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact kcck517@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when the game or its SDKs change; the effective date above shows the latest version, published on this page. Questions: kcck517@gmail.com.
