Kiln & Sand — Privacy Policy

Kiln & Sand Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026
Publisher / data controller: Muhammed Can Kocacık (individual developer), Türkiye
Contact: kcck517@gmail.com
App: Kiln & Sand (Turkish name: Kum Fırını), Android package com.kilnandsand.puzzle

Short version

  • The game has no account, no sign-in, no server of ours and no analytics of our own. We do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded videos, only when you ask for a bonus hint. These ads are provided by Google (AdMob, Google Mobile Ads SDK), which processes some device data to serve and measure ads. There are no banner, interstitial or forced ads.
  • An optional one-time purchase (Premium) is processed by Google Play, not by us.
  • Where the law requires it, the app asks for your ad-consent choice through Google's User Messaging Platform and lets you change it later.

Data stored on your device

The following stays in the app's private storage on your device and is not sent to us: game progress (levels completed, moves) and the unfinished level you can continue; settings (language, sound, haptics, reduced motion, pattern density, ceramic palette); hint and reward counters and random identifiers that stop the same ad reward from being granted twice (they are not linked to you); and the Premium purchase record (store transaction identifier and time) so Premium keeps working offline.

Deleting the app removes this data. If you have turned on your device's own backup (for example Android backup to your Google account), the operating system may include the app's saved files in that backup; that backup is controlled by you and the platform provider.

Advertising: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and consent

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show rewarded ads that you choose to watch. No other ad network or mediation partner is used. According to Google's disclosure for the Android SDK, the SDK collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), device and app identifiers (including the Android advertising ID where available), product interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, and diagnostic information. This data is encrypted in transit. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not give the SDK your name, email address or other identifying details.

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choices and your device settings. You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID in Android settings. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform: where a consent message is required in your region you see it on first launch, and where a privacy-options entry is required a "Privacy options" item in Settings lets you review or withdraw your choice. If you do not consent where consent is required, ads are not requested; hints that need a video are then unavailable and the game remains playable with the free hint, undo and restart.

Purchases (Premium)

Premium is a one-time, non-consumable purchase through Google Play Billing, accessed with Unity In-App Purchasing. Payment and card details are handled by Google; we never receive them. The app stores only the purchase record on your device. "Restore purchases" asks Google Play again whether you own Premium. There is no subscription and no account. Unity processes data collected by its purchasing library under the Unity Privacy Policy.

Permissions the Android app requests

Internet and network state (to load ads and reach the store), the advertising-ID permissions added by Google's ads libraries, billing (purchases), vibration (haptic feedback) and a wake-lock permission declared by SDK libraries. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Sharing, retention and your choices

We do not sell your data and do not run our own servers. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is shared with Google, which may process it in other countries under its own safeguards; purchase data is handled by Google. Data on your device stays until you delete the app or its data; data held by Google or Unity is retained under their policies.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the Turkish KVKK) you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, the practical controls are the in-app Privacy options (where shown), your device's advertising-ID controls, uninstalling the app, Google's own privacy tools and writing to kcck517@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data-protection authority.

Children

The game is a general-audience casual puzzle and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact kcck517@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when the game or its SDKs change; the effective date above shows the latest version, published on this page. Questions: kcck517@gmail.com.
